# murmrrr releases

Installers and auto-updates for [murmrrr](https://www.murmrrr.com), the hold-a-key dictation app for Windows and Mac. Hold a key, talk, and your words appear wherever your cursor is; everything runs on your own computer.

Download the latest version at [murmrrr.com/download](https://www.murmrrr.com/download). The app checks here for updates on its own.
